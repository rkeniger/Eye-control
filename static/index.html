Teach the eye

Stand somewhere in view, alone. Drag the pad until the eye looks straight at you, then save the point. Repeat at 3+ spread-out spots, including near and far.

    Detection zone

    People whose feet are outside the zone are ignored: not counted, and the eye won't look at them. Click the picture to add corners, drag a corner to move it, and double-click or right-click a corner to remove it. With fewer than 3 corners the whole picture counts.
